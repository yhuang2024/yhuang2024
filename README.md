## Welcome to my page!

Hi, I’m Yuyuan! I study Computer Science and International and Public Affairs at Brown University.

My interests lie in human-computer interaction. Some of my favorite topics within this field are socially responsible computing, technology policy, product/system design, AI/NLP, frontend development, data visualization, and robotics. I'm currently developing an AI-powered tool to help students learn legal cases for a GenAI law class and refining a Chrome extension for Courses @ Brown, a database of courses used by all students at Brown for registration.

This semester I'm taking courses on computer systems, human-computer interaction, and political economy. I'm also a robotics lead for Brown Space Engineering and conduct AI policy research for the Brown Human-Computer Interaction Lab under Dr. Jeff Huang and the Sociotechnical Wellbeing Research Lab under Dr. Diana Freed.

Outside of work, I like to write poetry (I've even published my own chapbook, which you can find [here](https://www.amazon.com/SWALLOW-Yuyuan-Huang/dp/B0D5V2GHDB/ref=sr_1_1?crid=1V0Q9CWUPLS8E&dib=eyJ2IjoiMSJ9.7DO97oswW4XB16M_m3HXKA.cG2OIEBxXxecK8eFX2JlJx7FFwVPfssEpyeOWJyjTj4&dib_tag=se&keywords=swallow+yuyuan+huang&qid=1779674812&sprefix=swallow+yuyuan+huan%2Caps%2C141&sr=8-1)!), read, take photos (see my profile picture from the Musée d'Orsay in Paris), hike, tinker/craft, and learn new languages.
